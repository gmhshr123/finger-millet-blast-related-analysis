Deletion events and genome features along E2

Move the mouse over the tracks to draw a vertical line through all of them; the box shows the values for that window. Click to pin a red line, and click it again to remove it. The arrow keys move a pinned line by one window. Select a chromosome to view it at full width.

What the values mean (per window)

All-base deletions
Fraction of the uniquely mappable bases that are deleted, mean of the 33 strains.
SNP-based deletions
Fraction of the SNP genotype calls (SNPs × 33 strains) that lie in runs of five or more missing SNPs.
Simple deletion, flanks joined
Number of deletion events in which reads join the two flanks directly.
TE at one or both ends
Number of deletion events with a TE joined to the flank at one or both ends.
SNP density
SNPs per kb of uniquely mappable sequence.
Repeat fraction
Fraction of the window annotated as repeat.
Effector genes
Number of effector genes whose midpoint lies in the window.
< 20% uniquely mappable
Windows in which less than 20% of the sequence is uniquely mappable. All-base deletions and SNP density are not shown for these windows.

An event is counted in every window it overlaps. Events in both groups are counted in both tracks.
